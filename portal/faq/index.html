Model FAQ

The answers, without the legal voice.

Access & Discretion

01
Who is the portal for?

Confirmed models and collaborators. Castings, concepts, unreleased sets: all of it is studio-confidential. It isn't public and it isn't for group chats.

How do I get in?

You get an access key with your call sheet. It opens your own ledger and nothing else. You can't see anyone else's numbers, and they can't see yours.

My key stopped working.

Message the studio and it gets reissued. Don't try variations, and don't ask another model for hers. A shared key is a revoked key.

Can I share what I see in here?

No. Castings carry creative direction and timing. A leak costs the launch, and the launch is what everyone gets paid from. If someone asks: wait for the drop.

Your Images

02
Do I get the RAW files?

No. RAWs stay in the studio archive. You get a curated gallery and the finished edits. That's standard anywhere that shoots editorially, and it keeps an issue looking like one issue.

Gallery, selects, finals — what's the difference?
  • Gallery. The curated set to review. Not everything shot.
  • Selects. What's being considered for the issue or the Vault.
  • Finals. Edited, approved, yours to post.
Can I post my photos?

Finals, yes — that's what they're for. Anything from an unreleased issue is on hold until it drops. When in doubt, ask; a quick yes is easier than undoing a leak.

When do I get them?

Within seven days of the shoot, delivered as a folder. Finals follow as the issue is laid out.

Castings

03
How do I get picked more often?
  • Answer fast when we ask about availability.
  • Arrive on time and camera-ready.
  • Bring a look you'd fight someone over.
  • Keep the concept to yourself until it drops.

That's genuinely it. The people who get called back are the ones who make the day easy.

What does the waitlist mean?

The call isn't fully open, or the spots are limited. You're considered as the schedule locks and slots move — which they always do.

Do I need an agency?

No. Every model in Issue 001 applied or was invited directly.

The Vault

04
What is the Vault?

The members' side of the site. Extended sets, alternate edits, the footage, the frames that didn't run in the issue. It's where royalties come from.

What does "Vault-approved" mean?

The studio has cleared a specific frame or clip for the Vault. Only approved content counts as a content unit, and only content units earn.

Is the Vault explicit?

Not by default. Vault just means members-only. There's a separate 21+ back room, and nothing of yours goes there without your say-so.

Can I say no to something going in?

Yes. Tell us the boundary and it's respected. Before the shoot, during it, or after you've seen the frames. Final approval sits with the studio, but a no from you is a no.

Money

05
How much do I make?

Your share of 40% of Vault revenue, in proportion to your approved content units. The full maths — and a calculator — is on the royalties page.

Can I see my numbers?

Yes — your portal shows your units, your share of the pool, and your accrued balance. For a full written statement, request one and name the quarter.

Why quarterly and not per shoot?

Vault revenue arrives continuously and content earns for as long as it's up. A quarter is short enough to feel real and long enough to be worth transferring.

What if I'm under $100?

It rolls to the next quarter automatically. Nothing expires and nothing is lost.

Does the shoot itself pay?

Shoots are trade. You get the images, the credit, and a share of what the work earns afterwards. Paid bookings are separate and stated up front when they exist.

On Set

06
What's expected?
  • On time, or an early message if you're not.
  • Hair and makeup done before arrival.
  • No surprise guests.
  • Nothing posted before the drop.
What if I'm uncomfortable?

Say so, or use the reset signal: tap either shoulder twice. Everything stops. No explanation required, no questions asked. There's also a private signal agreed with the host at the top of the day if you'd rather not say it out loud.

The best frames come from someone who feels safe. That isn't a courtesy, it's how the work gets good.

What do I bring?

One standout look minimum, a second if you want it. Accessories, heels, lip gloss, and anything that makes the look yours. Food and water are on us.

What am I signing?

A standard content release on arrival, granting PLAYER'S CLUB™ use of the images for editorial and promotional work. Ask for it in advance and you'll get it in advance. You should read it.

Anything not answered here — message the studio. There's no such thing as a stupid question about your own money or your own images.
