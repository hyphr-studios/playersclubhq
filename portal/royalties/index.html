The Math

Every quarter, exactly this
  1. The model pool is 40% of gross Vault revenue for the quarter.
  2. Count every Vault-approved content unit from every model.
  3. Unit value = the pool divided by that total.
  4. Your payout = unit value × your approved units.

That's the whole system. No popularity weighting, no discretionary bonuses, no favourites. Two models with the same number of approved units get paid the same. The math can't tell them apart.

The Guardrails

Non-negotiable
Approval

Only approved content counts

A frame counts once the studio approves it for the Vault. That protects you as much as it protects the brand. Nothing goes in that shouldn't.

Threshold

$100 minimum

Under a hundred, your balance rolls forward automatically. Nothing is lost. It just isn't worth a transfer yet.

Changes

Forward only

If the system ever changes, it applies to future quarters. Nothing is ever recalculated backwards.

Never

No follower maths

Likes, followers and reach never enter the formula. Contribution is the only input.

Balances and statements are kept by the studio. Your portal shows your current units and accrued balance. For a full statement, request one and name the quarter.
