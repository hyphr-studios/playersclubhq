The Process

Concept → Frame
01The Board

Every shoot starts as a wall of references. Light first, wardrobe second, location last. If the board doesn't move you, the camera won't.

02The Direction

One page, no more. What the story is, what it isn't, and the single frame we refuse to leave without.

03The Session

Closed set. Playlist on. We shoot past the plan until the plan looks obvious and the accident looks intentional.

04The Edit

Contact sheets marked by hand. Frames earn their place in the issue — the rest go to the Archive, or the Vault.

05The Sign-off

Nothing runs without the signature. When in doubt: would Stanley approve it?
