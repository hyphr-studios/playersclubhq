The Work

Editorial, not content

Cinematic shoots that become numbered issues. You style yourself. The frames are directed. What comes out is a magazine, not a camera roll.

What You Get

Images, and a share

Your gallery within seven days, credited. Then a cut of Vault revenue on every approved frame, split by what you shot. Never by follower count.

The Standard

Professional, always

Call sheets. Comfort signals. Food on set and a hard stop. Nobody gets pressured into anything, ever. 18+, and you can read the release before the day.

What we're shooting, when, and how many spots: that goes to applicants and to models in the portal. We don't publish briefs. Get accepted and you get a key, and the board opens up.

Put Your Name In

No agency required
Send Application → Opens your mail app with everything filled in. Attach three photos and send.

Three photos, please. One full length, one close, one however you want to be seen. Recent and unfiltered. Phone photos are fine. We're looking at presence, not retouching.

Applications are read by the studio and nothing is published without a signed release. If it's a fit you get a call sheet and a portal key. If it isn't this time you stay on file for the next one. Most of the SWIM cast were second looks.
